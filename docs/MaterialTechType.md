# Enum: MaterialTechType




_The physical matter or substance from which an object is made, including_

_any tangible substances, either naturally or synthetically derived,_

_ranging from specific materials to types of material designated by their_

_function. The term comprises both the media that are incorporated in an_

_object and any support to which the media are applied._

__

_Based on [AAT "materials (matter)"](http://vocab.getty.edu/page/aat/300010358);_

_[CDWA "Materials/Techniques"](http://www.getty.edu/research/publications/electronic_publications/cdwa/8materials.html#RTFToC2)_

__

_Use the most specific term for the material that is appropriate. It is_

_strongly recommended to take the indexing term from a published_

_controlled vocabulary, preferably a Linked Open Vocabulary, for example_

_the Art & Architecture Thesaurus, specifically its_

_[Materials hierarchy](http://www.getty.edu/vow/AATFullDisplay?find=materials&amp;logic=AND&amp;note=&amp;page=1&amp;subjectid=300010357)._

__

_-------------------------------------------------------------------------_

__

_Medium, as a value for the type attribute of the LIDO Term_

_Materials/Techniques element, designates the material that is applied to_

_a support in the making of an object or a work of art, such as ink on_

_paper or oil paint on canvas; often a particular technique or techniques_

_are closely associated with the media._

_Based on [AAT "media (artists' materials)"](http://vocab.getty.edu/page/aat/300163343);_

_[CDWA Materials/Techniques](https://www.getty.edu/research/publications/electronic_publications/cdwa/8materials.html#implement)._

__

_In many forms of art, a distinction is made between materials used as the_

_media, and materials used as the support. Medium is the material applied_

_to the support. For example, for a drawing described as pen and brown ink_

_and black chalk on paper, pen is the instrument, ink and black chalk are_

_the media, and paper is the support. [Cited from Categories for the_

_Description of Works of Art (CDWA) "Materials/Techniques"]_

__

_-------------------------------------------------------------------------_

__

_Support, as a value for the type attribute of the LIDO Term_

_Materials/Techniques element, designates the surface material to which_

_media are applied or a work is mounted, for example canvas, oak panel,_

_laid paper, wood, copper, glass, or marble. For example, with paintings,_

_drawings, prints, or photographs, the media would be applied to the_

_surface of a support such as paper, wood panel, canvas, or glass in order_

_to create the image. Used typically for two-dimensional works, although_

_the concept may apply to any work._

__

_Based on [AAT "supports"](http://vocab.getty.edu/page/aat/300014844);_

_[CDWA "Materials/Techniques"](https://www.getty.edu/research/publications/electronic_publications/cdwa/8materials.html#implement)_

__

_-------------------------------------------------------------------------_

__

_Record a term specifying the color, tint, or hue of the material,_

_particularly if color is an unusual or important characteristic of the_

_object. [All colored impressions of materials of an object are meant, not_

_just parts treated with dyestuff. The information refers to the overall_

_effect of color, not only as cause of, e.g., dyeing or painting.]_

__

_It is recommended to use internationally approved standard color charts,_

_codes and terminology such as the Universal Color Language (UCL). Use_

_preferably [color names as specified by the Art & Architecture Thesaurus](http://vocab.getty.edu/aat/300080438)_

_(AAT). Centroid color numbers, which refer to the appropriate_

_color-blocks on the_

_[Inter-Society Color Council and National Bureau of Standards (ISCC-NBS) color name charts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ISCC%E2%80%93NBS_system),_

_are also provided by the AAT._

__

_-------------------------------------------------------------------------_

__

_Implement, as a value for the type attribute of the LIDO Term_

_Materials/Techniques element, designates any object used to carry out the_

_technique in order to create or modify an object, including equipment,_

_tools, apparatus, utensils, instruments, and other things employed in any_

_trade or used in executing a task or work._

__

_Based on_

_[CDWA "Materials/Techniques"](http://www.getty.edu/research/publications/electronic_publications/cdwa/8materials.html#RTFToC2);_

_[AAT "implements"](http://vocab.getty.edu/page/aat/300404585)_

__

_-------------------------------------------------------------------------_

__

_Technique, as a value for the type attribute of the LIDO Term_

_Materials/Techniques element, designates the manner, method, or process_

_applied in the creation, design, or modification of an object._

__

_Based on [CDWA "Materials/Techniques"](http://www.getty.edu/research/publications/electronic_publications/cdwa/8materials.html#RTFToC2)._

__

_Use the most specific term for indexing that is appropriate. It is_

_strongly recommended to take the indexing term from a published_

_controlled vocabulary, preferably a_

_[Linked Open Vocabulary](http://lov.okfn.org/dataset/lov/), for example_

_the Art & Architecture Thesaurus, specifically its_

_[Processes and Techniques hierarchy](http://www.getty.edu/vow/AATHierarchy?find=techniques&amp;logic=AND&amp;note=&amp;page=1&amp;subjectid=300053001)._

__

_For information about scientific methods by which an object was found or_

_collected, such as field collection methods in archaeology, use the LIDO_

_Event Method element._

__

_-------------------------------------------------------------------------_

__

_Watermark, as a value for the type attribute of the LIDO Term_

_Materials/Techniques element, designates an identification of the_

_watermark type or name. A watermark is a design incorporated into paper_

_as it is made, typically a sign of the manufacturer, or a proof of_

_authenticity for documents and banknotes. The mark is visible through_

_transmitted light, appearing as translucent or shaded area._

__

_Based on [CDWA "Materials/Techniques"](http://www.getty.edu/research/publications/electronic_publications/cdwa/8materials.html#WatermarkIdentification);_

_[AAT "watermarks"](http://vocab.getty.edu/aat/300028749)_

__

_It is recommended to use as indexing terms descriptors of internationally_

_or nationally approved classifications, such as the_

_[Watermark Information System](http://www.wasserzeichen-online.de/wzis/index.php),_

_the [Index of watermark classes and subclasses](http://www.paperhistory.org/Standards/IPHN2.1.1_en.pdf),_

_provided as a_

_[Registration Standard](http://www.paperhistory.org/Standards/) of the_

_[International Association of Paper Historians (IPH)](http://www.paperhistory.org/index.php),_

_or the [Watermark Descriptor Tables](http://www.gravell.org/descriptors.php?&amp;&amp;offset=&amp;rectotal=&amp;query=),_

_provided by the_

_[Thomas L. Gravell Watermark Archive](http://www.gravell.org/)._

__
